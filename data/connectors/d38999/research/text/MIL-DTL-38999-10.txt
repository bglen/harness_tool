REQUIREMENTS:

  Dimensions and configuration: See figure 1.
  Interface dimensions shall conform to MIL-DTL-38999, except as noted.
  Material:
    Shell: Aluminum alloy as specified in MIL-DTL-38999 with finish B.
    Sealing gasket: Non-corrosive silicone or fluorosilicone elastomer.
  This receptacle mates with MS27473, MS27480, MS27484 and MS27500.
  Fits all polarization positions.

  Part or Identifying Number (PIN) example:

      M38999/10    -  10    B

          M38999/10      Specification sheet number
          10               Shell size
        B                Finish


 NOTE: The term PIN is equivalent to the term (part number, identification number, and type
  designator) which was previously used in this specification. In the PIN, a dash symbol (“-”) shall be
  placed between the specification sheet number and shell size.

Qualification:

  Qualification shall consist of the dynamic salt spray test, altitude immersion, coupling torque, coupling
  pin strength, and visual and mechanical inspection as specified in MIL-DTL-38999, conducted on two
  samples of each shell size.
