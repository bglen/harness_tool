REQUIREMENTS:

  Dimensions and configuration: See figure 1. Interface dimensions shall be in accordance with
  MIL-DTL-38999.

  This connector mates with MIL-DTL-38999/20, /21, /22, /23, /24, /25 and /27.

  For insert arrangements: See MIL-STD-1560.

  Connector accessories: SAE-AS85049.

  Part or Identifying Number (PIN) example:

       D38999/       26   W   A     35     P    N

          D38999/  DoD number prefix
          26          Specification sheet number
      W         Class
        A           Shell size code
          35          Insert arrangement
        P          Contact style
       N           Polarizing positions (N is required for normal position)


  Class F is not for Navy use and is inactive for Air Force new design use.

 NOTE: The term PIN is equivalent to the term (part number, identification number, and type
  designator) which was previously used in this specification.

  Note: Bending moment torque (class J and M). Details of bending moment torque requirements may be
  found in MIL-DTL-38999/20. Plug requirements were set at the requirements of the flange mounted
  receptacles (MIL-DTL-38999/20, representing the strongest mating partner for the plug).
