REQUIREMENTS:
  Dimensions and configuration: See figure 1.
  Interface dimensions shall conform to MIL-DTL-38999, except one design shall accommodate all
  polarization positions (within its shell size).
  This receptacle mates with MIL-DTL-38999/46 and /47.
  Shell material and finish shall be as specified in MIL-DTL-38999.

    Qualification (all classes). Qualification shall be in accordance with MIL-DTL-38999, conducted on
